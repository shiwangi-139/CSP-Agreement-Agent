1. The idea in five minutes

1.1 The business

State Bank of India (SBI) offers banking in villages and small towns through Customer Service Points (CSPs), also called kiosks or Business Correspondent agents. A CSP is a local shopkeeper who opens accounts, takes deposits, pays withdrawals and sends money for the bank's customers. Eko India Financial Services is the company that recruits, manages and supports about 490 such CSPs for SBI. Every CSP has a unique KO code (for example 1A852163).

Each CSP is looked after by an RM (Relationship Manager, an Eko employee) and a DC (District Coordinator, who visits the CSPs in a district).

1.2 The problem

To keep a kiosk working, the bank requires three documents to be on file and valid:

DocumentWhat it isHow long it is valid
CSP AgreementThe contract between the CSP and Eko (about 13 pages, often on stamp paper).As written in it (often 2 or 3 years); 1 year if nothing is written.
Police Verification (PVR / character certificate)Proof from the police that the CSP has no criminal record.As written (6 months or 1 year); 1 year if nothing is written.
IIBF certificateThe CSP passed the Indian Institute of Banking & Finance exam for BC agents.Does not expire.

When a document expires or is missing, the bank can stop the kiosk. Then the CSP earns no commission, customers lose their local bank, and Eko loses revenue. Before this agent, documents came by e-mail and WhatsApp in every possible shape (phone photos, scans, Hindi, handwriting), and people had to read each one and chase each CSP by phone.

1.3 What the agent does

Collect→Read→Decide→ Remind→Escalate→Learn
  1. Collect: it reads the documents from two Gmail inboxes, from each CSP's personal upload link, and from the DC master link (one link a DC uses for all CSPs).
  2. Read: it reads each page (also photos and Hindi) and finds the type of document, the issue date, the validity and whose document it is.
  3. Decide: it accepts the document, refuses it with a clear reason, or sends it to a person when it is not sure.
  4. Remind: it sends the CSP a WhatsApp message with their own upload link, on a fixed schedule, until the documents arrive.
  5. Escalate: it sends each RM and DC a daily list of their CSPs that are still pending.
  6. Learn: every night it checks its own work, finds its mistakes, and helps people fix the rules.
Golden rule: the agent tells a CSP "accepted" or "expired" only when it is sure. Anything uncertain goes to a person first. A wrong "accepted" is treated as ten times worse than a wrong refusal.

3. Daily tasks step by step

3.1 Start of the day (5 minutes)

  1. Open Today: look at the goal band (how many CSPs are fully compliant and the pace).
  2. Work the Needs attention list top-down; each row has its button (call, open, take the file…).
  3. Open the Review queue and clear it (3.2).
  4. Admins: open the Agent Coach and read the "Fix now" findings.

3.2 Decide a document in the Review queue

  1. Open the item. Read the reason at the top (why it waits) and the box "How the agent read it": the dates it found, the exact text each came from, what it ignored, its risk and every date printed on the document.
  2. Click the file to see the document itself.
  3. If the rules have been improved since it arrived, press Re-read with current rules.
  4. Check the issue date (and the validity offered) against the document. Change them if needed.
  5. Press Accept (it counts now; the CSP is told if they uploaded it themselves) or Reject document (it does not count; the CSP is asked again).
Kinds of items: Safety check (the agent held it: something does not add up) · Risk check (counted, but please look) · Validity check (the 1-year default was used; set the years if the document states them) · DC upload (a DC sent it through the master link) · Spot check / Reference check (random or chosen checks that measure the agent's accuracy).

3.3 A CSP says "I uploaded, but it was refused"

  1. Open Form uploads & DC link, find the CSP, and press view what they sent.
  2. If the document is in fact right, an admin presses take this file: it is saved for the CSP and waits in the Review queue for your Accept.

3.4 Send a CSP their link or a reminder

Open the CSP and press Send reminder now (WhatsApp with their upload link) or Create upload link (to copy and share yourself).

3.5 Messages waiting for approval

In review mode every message waits in the Communication Hub: Approve, Edit text (e-mail only: WhatsApp always uses the Meta-approved layout) or Reject. In auto mode the agent sends by itself within the allowed hours and daily limit.

3.6 The DC master link (admins)

On Form uploads & DC link: copy the link to share with DCs, bring back the last shared link, make a new one (the old one stops), or switch it off. From the same card: Download DC Excel, Text summary, Daily progress (all DCs) with "Received on <day>" per DC, and the key for the DCs' live Google Sheets.

3.7 CSP questions

Open CSP questions, call or message the CSP, then press Mark answered. A CSP who gets no answer often stops trying.

3.8 Emergency: stop all messages

Admins: Monitoring → Rules & safety → Pause all sending, with a reason. Nothing goes out until Resume sending.

3.9 The Agent Coach and Rules & safety (admins)

  • Coach findings say what to improve, with numbers, a suggested fix, example CSPs and a button. When you fixed something, press Fix applied and write what you did: the next nights check whether it is really gone.
  • Rules & safety lists every reading rule with its health. A rule on probation holds its new readings for a person; press Release if its "mistakes" were wrong answers, or Hold its readings to stop a rule yourself.

3.10 For CSPs and DCs: the upload page

The CSP opens the link from WhatsApp (no password), sees which documents are still needed, and for each one either takes a photo of every page or chooses a PDF, then types the issue date. Photos are checked on the phone (blur, darkness, size) and joined into one PDF. The page answers at once: accepted, being checked by our team, or refused with the reason and what to do. There is also a "Have a question?" box and a field to correct contact details (a person approves each change). A DC uses the same page through the master link, choosing the CSP by KO code.

4. How the agent decides: rules for each document

The agent reads with written rules (like a checklist), not with guesses. Each rule was taught from real documents. When no rule fits, a person decides. An AI model may suggest a date for a person, but never decides.

4.1 Steps for every file

  1. File check: only PDF, JPG or PNG; at most 10 MB and 25 pages; photos too blurry, dark or small are sent back with what to fix.
  2. Reading the text: digital PDFs are read directly; scans and photos by OCR (Tesseract, English + Hindi). If OCR is unsure, a second engine (RapidOCR) reads too. If the key line is still missing, the agent re-reads the area around the important words, with stamp ink removed, and finally reads the first pages whole with the second engine.
  3. Which document is it? Only Agreement, PVR and IIBF are allowed. Aadhaar, PAN and others are not stored. PVR application receipts and IIBF fee receipts are recognised as "not the certificate".
  4. Whose is it? The CSP name or KO code on the document is checked. A document showing another CSP goes to a person.
  5. Dates: the issue date and the validity, by the rules below.
  6. Safety gate: a risk score decides accept / accept and check / hold for a person (section 8).

4.2 Dates in general

  • Dates are found in many forms: 20/04/2024, 2024-05-12, 13-Feb-2025, 08th JUL 2022, "13th day of February 2025", Hindi digits and Hindi month names. OCR slips are repaired (o→0, S→5, l→1, a slash read as "I" or "1", "9026" read for "2026").
  • An issue date cannot be in the future or before 2010.
  • Phone camera stamps ("Shot on vivo… Oct 24, 2026, 17:13") are ignored, and shown as ignored.

4.3 CSP Agreement

4.4 Police Verification (PVR / character certificate)

QuestionRule

Application receiptA receipt, challan or "status: registered" is the application, not the certificate. On the upload page it is noted ("applied on …") and the link stays open for the certificate; it does not count as a PVR.

4.5 IIBF certificate

Issue date from "DATED …", else the digital signature, else the first date. The holder's name and registration number are read. It never expires. Fee receipts and admit cards are not the certificate. A second copy of the same certificate (same number or date) is not stored again.

4.6 When the CSP types a date on the upload page

  • A date is required and cannot be in the future.
  • If the document's own date can be read, the document wins; a difference is shown to the reviewer.
  • If the date cannot be read, the typed date is used and a person checks it.
  • "Expired" is told to the CSP only when the read date equals the typed date and the validity was not guessed; otherwise a person checks first.

4.8 The DC master link

One link for all working CSPs. It never expires; only "make a new link" or "switch off" ends it. It refuses a document that is still valid for more than 60 days, a file already filed for another CSP, the same document as the one on file, and a document showing another CSP's code. Everything stored from it is also listed in the Review queue as "DC upload".

8. Safety: context, harness, guardrails and feedback loop

Context = what the agent looks at before it decides. Harness = the checks and limits around every decision and message. Guardrails = things the agent is never allowed to do. Feedback loop = how it learns from its mistakes.

8.1 Context: what each decision sees

  • Eko's rules (defaults, page count, what counts as a certificate).
  • The document's evidence: every date printed on it with its kind (issue, signing, start, digital signature, end, birth, camera stamp), the text around it, which one was used, how well the pages were read.
  • The rules' health: whether the rule that read it is healthy, on watch or on probation.
  • The CSP's record: other copies of the document, earlier corrections, the calling sheet.
  • A plain-words "why" is saved with every reading and shown to the reviewer.

8.2 Harness: the safety gate (risk score)

PointsReason
100Impossible dates (expiry before issue, issued after it arrived, valid over 6 years) · a rule on probation read it
70Another copy with the same issue date was read with another expiry
35The 1-year default was used but an end date is printed
15 / 15 / 15Another issue date printed · a rule on watch · the CSP's dates were corrected before
10 / 10Pages not read or low confidence · expiry within 7 days of today

60 or more: hold (waits for a person, does not count, CSP told "being checked") · 30–59: check (counts, a person looks) · below 30: accept.

8.3 Harness: rules on probation (circuit breaker)

Every night each reading rule is scored against the nightly reading check and people's corrections. A rule that accepts wrongly goes on probation: its new readings wait for a person until it is fixed (released by itself after a clean check) or an admin releases it. After every code update a release check compares the reading with the version before; rules that newly accept wrongly are put on probation at once. Policy defaults are never put on probation. The setting RULE_BREAKER=watch turns holding off (only shows).

8.4 Guardrails: what the agent never does

  • Never tells a CSP "expired" unless sure; never accepts on an AI model's word alone.
  • Never messages a number that is not on the calling sheet; never messages an Inactive CSP; never sends free text on WhatsApp.
  • Never invents a CSP from an e-mail; never stores Aadhaar or PAN; never writes to the calling sheet.
  • Never sends from or changes the second Gmail inbox (read-only).
  • Every data-fix script runs as a dry run first; nothing changes without --apply.
  • A person can stop all sending with one button.

8.5 Feedback loop: how it learns

StepWhat happens
1 NoticeEvery night it finds 10 kinds of warning signs in its own records (e.g. refused then accepted, a person corrected it, readings that disagree, impossible results).
2 ExplainThe Agent Coach turns them into findings with numbers, a suggested fix and examples.
3 FixA developer fixes the rule with a test from the real document. Before it goes live, a shadow test reads every decided document with the new code and says "safe to put live" or not.
4 ProveThe next night's reading check and release check show what got fixed and whether anything broke.
5 FollowEach finding is followed: open → fix applied → verified, or reopened if it comes back.
6 LearnEach night a few documents whose confirmation teaches the most go to a person; each answer joins the answer set of the reading check.

A file that was unreadable is read again with the current rules whenever it is sent again, so better rules rescue old documents.

9.3 Planned: secure sign-in with Google

Status: plan, to be approved.
  • Invite-only: there is no "create account". An admin adds a person (name, e-mail, role) first; only those people can sign in.
  • "Sign in with Google" button. The agent checks Google's signed answer itself: it must be meant for this dashboard, recent, and for a verified e-mail. For Eko staff it must be a company account (the hosted domain eko.co.in, checked with Google's hd value, not just the text of the address).

12. Questions people ask

Does an AI decide whether a CSP's document is accepted?

No. Written rules decide, and a person decides whenever the rules are not sure. A local AI model may suggest a date for a person to check.

Can the agent message the wrong person?

Only numbers listed on the calling sheet for that CSP (or their RM/DC) can receive a message, and this is checked again just before sending.

What happens when a document expires?

Reminders start before expiry (60 days for agreements), the CSP is asked on WhatsApp with their link, then the RM and DC are told. The label shows "renewal overdue" until a new document is accepted.

A CSP sent the same document again. Why was it accepted this time?

A file that was unreadable before is read again with today's rules; the rules may have learned its wording since.

Why does a document say "being checked by our team"?

The agent was not sure (risk, unknown wording, a typed date that differs, or a DC upload). A person decides in the Review queue.

Can I see why the agent decided something?

Yes: every document has "How the agent read it", with the text each date came from, what was ignored, and the risk.

Who can see CSPs' documents?

Admins, and each RM for their own CSPs. CSPs only see their own link. Every dashboard action is recorded.
